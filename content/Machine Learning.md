A subset of [[AI]]. It's about developing algorithms that allow computers to learn from and make decisions based upon **data**. Rather than being explicitly programmed to perform a specific task, these algorithms use statistical techniques to make predictions from data without human intervention. 



A very broad term containing:
* [[Supervised Learning]]
* [[Unsupervised Learning]]
* [[Reinforcement Learning]]
Examples:
* [[Deep Learning]]
* [[Support Vector Machines]]
* [[Regression]]
* [[Random Forest]]
