Speech-to-text

Leverages [[Neural Networks|neural network]] for speech recognition. 
Analyses speech patterns from voice samples and text. it then uses this knoedge to convert new recordings into acurate text.

Allows:
* real-time transcription of voice commands
	* Siri 
* Enables dictation 
	* Youtube uses it for automatic captioning.
* Provides transcription services 
* Voice search
