Two main components: 
1. Generator 
	* generates new data samples 
	* creates data that looks real
2. Discriminator 
	* verifies generated data (reinforces learning) until it cannot differentiate between the generated data and real data. 
Uses the [[Reinforcement Learning]] paradigm


Uses:
* Image synthesis 
* Style transfer 
* Data Augmentation
