In [algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebra "Algebra"), a **homomorphism** is a [structure-preserving](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morphism "Morphism") [map](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Map_\(mathematics\) "Map (mathematics)") between two [algebraic structures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraic_structure "Algebraic structure") of the same type (such as two [groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Group_\(mathematics\) "Group (mathematics)"), two [rings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ring_\(mathematics\) "Ring (mathematics)"), or two [vector spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vector_space "Vector space")).
